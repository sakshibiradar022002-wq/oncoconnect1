How It Works

1

Get the App for Your Role

Doctor: install the Windows software. Lab & Patient: install the Android app (or scan its QR). The VELTRUVIA cloud is already running for your clinic — every app connects to it automatically, no addresses or setup needed.

2

Sign In

Your login is created for you — doctors get theirs from the clinic admin, patients and labs receive a Welcome Card with credentials. Type it in once and you're in.

3

Everything Stays in Sync

A symptom logged on a phone appears in the Doctor software instantly. Lab results reach the patient the moment they're uploaded. All data is saved on the server.
