ℹ️ How It Works

VELTRUVIA uses a local blockchain to create an immutable audit trail. Every medical record operation is hashed and recorded on-chain, providing tamper-proof verification. The actual encrypted records remain in the secure database — only their cryptographic fingerprints are stored on the blockchain.
